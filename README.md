# AleksMouseTester is now InputLab

**AleksMouseTester has moved into [InputLab](https://github.com/moneks1106-code/InputLab): one free Windows app for your mouse, your controllers and the PC they run on. This repository is no longer updated.**

### ➡️ [Download InputLab](https://github.com/moneks1106-code/InputLab/releases/latest)

## Everything from AleksMouseTester, in InputLab

- The mouse quick test with its delivery score, now fairer for new runs (score v2): a gap where you moved too slowly for your rate and DPI no longer counts against the mouse.
- Timeline, Compare with the sweet spot, and the power-saving check with Restore.
- The same honesty: InputLab measures how evenly your mouse's reports reach Windows (host-delivery timing), not the mouse's internal polling and not click-to-photon latency.

## New in InputLab

- **Controllers** in the same app: everything from AleksControllerTester.
- **Home:** your PC at a glance, every rating with its source.
- **USB timing:** a mouse run seen at the USB controller and in the app on one clock.
- **System › Interrupts:** which CPU core holds up your mouse, measured, and a drag-and-drop interrupt layout you can undo.
- **During this run:** what else ran on your PC during a mouse run.
- The USB map, the power settings and a wireless mouse doctor.

## Your old runs come with you

In InputLab, **Mouse › Compare › Import from AleksMouseTester** copies the runs AleksMouseTester saved. The old files are only read, never changed, and AleksMouseTester can stay installed next to InputLab.

## Old versions

The AleksMouseTester releases stay on this page for reference; v0.2.16 was the last one. They get no more updates. Please report problems and results in [InputLab's issues](https://github.com/moneks1106-code/InputLab/issues).
